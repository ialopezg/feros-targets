documentation. Firmware binaries, operating-system images, ROM collections,

attribution. Preserve notices and verify redistribution rights before inclusion.

permission to redistribute its contents.

source material; they do not imply endorsement or affiliation. Naming an
external component does not bundle it or change its license.

and distinguish development dependencies from material actually distributed.
